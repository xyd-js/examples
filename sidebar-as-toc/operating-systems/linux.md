# Linux

Linux is a family of open-source Unix-like operating systems based on the
Linux kernel, first released by Linus Torvalds in 1991.

## Distributions

Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, and Arch are among the most widely used
distributions, each with its own package manager and release cadence.

## Where it runs

From embedded devices and phones to most of the world's servers and all of
the TOP500 supercomputers.
