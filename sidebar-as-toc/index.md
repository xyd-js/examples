# Sidebar as Table of Contents

Every item under **Operating Systems** and **Programming Languages** in the
sidebar is a section of THIS page — those groups declare `asToc: true`, so
their markdown files are stitched together here instead of becoming standalone
pages. Click a sidebar item to scroll to its section; scroll the page and the
sidebar highlights the section you are reading. The right-hand table of
contents is hidden — the sidebar has taken its job.

The **Resources** group below them is a normal group: its items navigate to
real pages.
