# Nested sidebar dropdown

This example shows an `appearance: "sidebarDropdown"` segment whose entries
can be **nested**: instead of pointing at a single `page`, an entry declares
its own `pages: [...]` — it renders as an inline-expandable group inside the
dropdown, with icons and descriptions on both group rows and children.

Head to [Getting Started](/platform/docs/getting-started) and open the
switcher at the top of the sidebar.
